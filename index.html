Small games, big fun.

A pocket arcade of eight clean, fast mini-games — Snake, 2048, Memory, Minesweeper, Breakout, Tic-Tac-Toe, Simon and a reaction test. Keyboard and touch friendly, best scores saved on your device, no ads and no tracking.

Eight games, no setup

Everything runs in the page — open a game, play, and close it with Esc. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

Pick a game.

Eight classics, one page. Arrow keys, WASD, mouse and touch all work, and your best scores are remembered locally.

Good to know

Built to be played.

Keyboard friendly

Every game is playable without a mouse: arrows or WASD to move, Space to act, Enter to restart and Esc to close.

Works offline

No servers, no accounts and no network calls — once the page is loaded the games keep working even without a connection.

Private by default

Your best scores live in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and the same light or dark theme follows you across the site.
